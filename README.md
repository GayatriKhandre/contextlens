# ContextLens

## Think deeper. Decide better.

ContextLens is a structured AI thinking partner for difficult decisions.

Instead of immediately telling you what to do, ContextLens helps you understand
the situation first. It separates facts from unknowns, identifies assumptions
and constraints, asks useful clarification questions, explores perspectives,
connects related factors, challenges blind spots, compares options and
trade-offs, and finally helps the human make their own decision.

**The AI does not make the decision for you.**

---

## Why ContextLens?

Many difficult decisions are not simply information problems.

They are **context problems**.

A person may be making a decision while:

- missing important information
- confusing assumptions with facts
- overlooking another person's perspective
- ignoring constraints
- focusing on only one interpretation
- jumping to a solution too early

Most AI assistants are optimized to answer quickly.

ContextLens is designed to do something different:

> **Make the reasoning visible before making the decision.**

---

## Built for a real situation

ContextLens was designed around a problem faced by a friend who is a fresher
working in a software role.

Her workload had become very high. She was handling multiple tasks,
sometimes working beyond normal hours, and had very little time or energy
left to prepare for a future job switch.

At the same time, she was worried that, as a fresher, pushing back on her
manager could create problems.

The obvious AI response would be:

> "Quit your job."

or:

> "Just start preparing for interviews."

But neither answer understands the complete situation.

The real question is:

> **What is actually known, what is missing, what assumptions are being made,
> and what realistic options exist?**

That is the problem ContextLens is designed to explore.
